Our Thesis

About

DeFi is full of broken plumbing. We find the leaks and fix them.

Most teams chase features. We hunt friction. For five years, we've lived inside DeFi—not watching from the sidelines, but using it, stress-testing it, feeling where it breaks. Every product we build starts with a problem we've experienced ourselves.

How We Work

Process

01

Find the friction

We don't brainstorm in conference rooms. We operate inside protocols, talk to power users, and track where value disappears.

02

Design from physics

Before writing code, we map the forces at play—incentives, attack vectors, user behaviors. Solutions that ignore dynamics fail.

03

Grow, don't force

We launch lean, iterate with early users, and let adoption happen organically. Forced growth creates fragile systems.

What We Believe

Principles

01

Privacy isn't optional

Every indexed transaction is leverage against the individual. Financial surveillance enables control. We build tools that can't be weaponized.

02

Emergence beats force

Complex systems resist top-down mandates. Products that require artificial push eventually collapse. We design for organic adoption.

03

Simple surface, deep structure

The best technology feels inevitable—like it was always obvious. Complexity should exist in the architecture, never in the interface.

04

Skin in the game

We use what we build. We hold what we believe in. When your incentives align with your users, the product takes care of itself.
