About / Thesis

Experience

Years in DeFi

We identify bottlenecks in decentralised finance and build products to solve them.

BLOOM is a for-profit venture studio operating at the intersection of physics and nature. We see DeFi not as a collection of protocols, but as an emergent field—complex systems where small interventions can cascade into fundamental shifts. Our thesis: find the friction, build the solution, let it bloom.
