# Article Writing Guidelines

Style guide for naly.dev research articles and X threads. Modeled on "The Jackpot Age" and related writing.

---

## Voice & Tone

**Authoritative but accessible.** You're explaining complex systems to smart people who haven't seen this angle before. No hand-holding, no dumbing down. Assume intelligence, deliver insight.

- Present tense, active voice
- Second person when addressing reader ("You're not investing. You're gambling.")
- First person sparingly for personal stakes or conviction
- Casual vocabulary, serious ideas

---

## Formatting Rules

### Punctuation

**No em dashes.** Use these alternatives:
- Colons for emphasis or expansion
- Parentheses for asides
- Periods for stronger breaks
- Semicolons for related clauses

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|-------------|
| Money has always existed as screen numbers—for this generation especially | Money has always existed as screen numbers. For this generation especially. |
| The answer—hyper-gambling—became their escape | The answer: hyper-gambling. It became their escape. |

### Sentence Structure

**No "It's not X, it's Y" constructions.** Rephrase with "rather than" or restructure entirely.

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|-------------|
| It's not greed, it's self-defense | This isn't greed. It's self-defense against systemic barriers. |
| It's not about the money, it's about freedom | The goal isn't money. It's freedom. |
| It's not investing, it's gambling | Call it what it is: gambling dressed as investing. |

Better patterns:
- "X rather than Y"
- "The real driver is X, not Y"
- "Forget X. Focus on Y."
- State the truth directly without the negation dance

## Hero Image Spec

Standard spec for X article hero/card images. Reference implementation: `hero-buying-panic.html`.

- **Canvas:** 1200×480px (5:2 ratio) for X article cards
- **Background:** Full-bleed image, `object-fit: cover`, 50% opacity over `#050608`
- **Overlay:** Radial gradient (center light → edge dark) instead of directional
- **Vignette:** `inset box-shadow 200px`
- **Text layout:** Centered (flexbox column, `align-items: center; justify-content: center`)
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk — line 1 at 42px/500 weight (muted), line 2 at 140px/700 weight (bright)
- **Handle:** `@defi_naly` top-right, 15px, muted

## Structure

### Sections

- **Short, punchy section headers** (2-4 words)
- **One idea per section** (the Rule of One)
- **Lead with questions** when introducing a new angle ("Why does this gamble appeal?")
- **Blockquotes for key insights** (max 1-2 per article)

### Paragraphs

- 1-3 sentences max
- White space is your friend
- Front-load the point; don't bury it

### Lists

- Use sparingly for scannable content
- Each item should be parallel in structure
- Highlight one key item with accent styling when appropriate

## Word-Level Guidelines

### Cut These Words

- Very, really, extremely, incredibly (weak intensifiers)
- Just, actually, basically (filler)
- In order to (use "to")
- Things, stuff (vague)
- Innovative, robust, seamless, cutting-edge (corporate fluff)

### AI-Speak to Avoid

These phrases scream "generated by AI." Never use them.

**Throat-clearing openers:**
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "Interestingly..."
- "Let's dive in..."
- "In today's world..."
- "In this day and age..."
- "With that being said..."

**Empty validation:**
- "Great question!"
- "Absolutely!"
- "That's a really interesting point"

**Overused AI verbs:**
- Delve, dive, unpack
- Navigate, leverage
- Unlock, empower
- Explore (when not literal)

**Corporate buzzwords:**
- Game-changer, revolutionary
- Paradigm shift
- Holistic, ecosystem
- Synergy, optimize
- Actionable insights
- Best practices
- Move the needle

**Hedging phrases:**
- "It goes without saying..."
- "Needless to say..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "Moving forward..."
- "When it comes to..."

**Structure tells:**
- Starting with "So," or "Well,"
- "In conclusion..."
- "To summarize..."
- Numbered lists for everything
- Excessive use of "First... Second... Third..."

**The fix:** State things directly. If you wouldn't say it out loud to a friend, cut it.

### Replace Weak Verbs

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| Utilize | Use |
| Implement | Set up |
| Leverage | Use |
| Facilitate | Help |

### Specificity Over Abstraction

| Vague | Specific |
|-------|----------|
| Save time | Save 4 hours every week |
| Many customers | 2,847 teams |
| Fast results | Results in 14 days |
| Improve your workflow | Cut your reporting time in half |

## The Seven Sweeps (Before Publishing)

Run these passes in order. After each sweep, re-check previous ones.

1. **Clarity:** Can the reader understand what you're saying?
2. **Voice & Tone:** Is the copy consistent in how it sounds?
3. **So What:** Does every claim answer "why should I care?"
4. **Prove It:** Is every claim supported with evidence?
5. **Specificity:** Is the copy concrete enough to be compelling?
6. **Heightened Emotion:** Does the copy make the reader feel something?
7. **Zero Risk:** Have we removed every barrier to action?

## Example Patterns (from The Jackpot Age)

### Opening Hook
> "It's all just a number on the screen... until it isn't."

Short. Provocative. Sets the frame.

### Insight Callout
> **"The path you take to make 'number go up' shapes you far more than the numbers themselves."**

Bold the core insight. Use quotes if it's a direct statement.

### Concrete Scene Setting
> Picture an $18 million mansion hosting influencers and traders. Upstairs features Love Island-style content creation; downstairs contains traders livestreaming their P&L to audiences while DJs provide soundtrack.

Paint the picture. Make abstract ideas tangible.

### Framework Delivery
> Hyper-gambling quietly erodes three critical freedom components:
> - **Time consumption:** Every waking moment ties to market volatility
> - **Mental taxation:** Nervous systems experience constant fight-or-flight
> - **Path dependency:** Futures become contingent on low-probability outcomes

Number them. Name them. Make them memorable.

### Closing Wisdom
> "The money game isn't about finding the right lottery ticket. It's about building a life where you no longer need one."

End with quotable truth. Something worth sharing.

## Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] No em dashes anywhere
- [ ] No "it's not X, it's Y" constructions
- [ ] No AI-speak phrases (delve, dive, unpack, leverage, etc.)
- [ ] No throat-clearing openers ("It's worth noting...")
- [ ] Every section answers "so what?"
- [ ] Claims have evidence or are clearly marked as opinion
- [ ] Vague words replaced with specific ones
- [ ] Read aloud for voice consistency
- [ ] Core insight is bold and quotable
- [ ] Ending leaves reader with clear takeaway
- [ ] Sounds like a human wrote it
