DeFi strategist (Balancer, Beets Fi), 14.3K X followers. Building at the intersection of DeFi, privacy, and AI. 12-month runway to reposition and find next role.
Core Thesis
"Privacy is becoming foundational infrastructure, not a feature."
Three forces converging:
1. Vitalik says on-chain governance requires anonymous voting to work.
2. Altman says every company becomes an API company, with AI agents calling services dynamically.
3. AI agents transacting on-chain create fully legible behavior patterns. They need private financial rails.

Nobody is consistently connecting these dots publicly. That's the gap to own.

Content Rules
  • Thesis first, product second. Never lead with TIPZ screenshots.
  • Separate insight posts from product posts. Insight builds reputation, product spends it.
  • QT ecosystem news only when there's a natural connection. Add the privacy angle nobody else is providing.
  • Every post should work standalone. Don't rely on people clicking through.
  • Lead with an insight, let TIPZ be supporting evidence.
