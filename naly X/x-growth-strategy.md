By month 6: clear monetization path identified and tested.

"Framework thinker who does the math, admits the risk, and writes like a human."

This sits in the gap between:
- Chart traders (all technicals, no thesis)
- Macro pontificators (all theory, no skin in the game)
- Shills (all conviction, no math)
- AI content mills (all volume, no voice)

You have something none of them do: a real voice, real stakes, and the tools to back it up (naly.dev).

### Three Moats

1. **The Voice** — sardonic, arena-not-sidelines, profanity as punctuation. Impossible to fake. (See `voice-guide.md`)
2. **The Math Habit** — every thesis reduced to ratios and gap analyses. Screenshottable numbers, not vibes.
3. **The Visuals** — "Alpine Patience" data cards and naly.dev interactive tools. Nobody else has this. (See `design-philosophy.md`)

### Two-Platform Strategy

X and Substack serve different roles. They're not redundant. They compound each other.

| Platform | Role | Strength | Weakness |
|----------|------|----------|----------|
| X | Discovery + reach | Algorithmic distribution, virality, reply strategy | Ephemeral, no depth, no ownership |
| Substack | Depth + ownership | Long-form, email list (you own it), monetizable | No discovery, stalls without external traffic |

**The flywheel:** X drives discovery → Substack captures depth-seekers → Substack articles get repurposed back to X → repeat.

X is the top of funnel. Substack is the asset you own. If X disappeared tomorrow, the Substack list is yours. Build both.

**Daily constants (every day, 20 min):**
- 3-5 substantive replies on larger accounts' posts
- Engage genuinely in 1-2 community threads (Zcash, Hyperliquid, macro)

### Format Ratios (X)

- 60% single tweets (punchy, one idea, < 280 chars ideal)
- 20% visual data cards (Alpine Patience aesthetic)
- 10% quote tweets (add the contrarian angle or the math nobody did)
- 10% threads (rare, only for article-tier ideas that don't warrant full Substack)

### The Two-Week Content Cycle

**Week A: Article Week**
- 3 hours: Write Substack article (one pillar, one thesis)
- 2 hours: Extract 8-12 derivative pieces for X (see repurposing engine below)
- Result: 1 article + 8-12 tweets pre-loaded for the week

**Week B: Tweet Week**
- Daily posts from pillar rotation
- Focus on reply strategy and community engagement
- Scout next article topic based on what performed

**Monthly output target:** 2 Substack articles + ~50 X posts (tweets, QTs, visuals combined) + 100+ replies

### The Repurposing Engine

One Substack article generates 8-12 derivative X posts:

1. **The hook** — opening paragraph as standalone tweet
2. **The math** — key ratio/number as a data tweet
3. **The quotable** — best single line, formatted for screenshot
4. **The story** — one narrative anecdote extracted
5. **The framework** — the named mental model as a standalone
6. **The contrarian** — the most surprising claim, no context
7. **The poll** — turn a thesis into a question
8. **The visual** — one data card from the article's numbers
9. **The follow-up** — "people asked about X, here's more"
10. **The thread** — 3-5 tweet summary of the full article (rare, for best pieces)
11. **The callback** — reference the article weeks later when news validates it
12. **The update** — revisit with new data when numbers change

This is how 2 articles/month become 50+ posts. Volume without grinding.

### Substack Revival Plan

The Substack stalled because of inconsistent publishing. The algorithm punishes gaps. Fix:

- **Weeks 1-4:** Publish weekly (4 posts). Over-deliver to re-train the algorithm and re-engage dormant subscribers.
- **Weeks 5+:** Settle into biweekly (2/month). Consistent > frequent.
- **Every X article-derivative tweet:** Include "full breakdown on Substack" CTA with link.
- **Every Substack article:** End with "follow on X for daily takes" CTA.
- **Pin a Substack link in X bio.** Always.
- **Cross-post excerpts as Substack Notes** (Substack's social layer) for additional discovery.

## 4. Growth Mechanics (8 Tactics)

### 1. "Show Your Math" Reply Strategy

The highest-ROI growth tactic on X. 3-5 substantive replies per day on larger accounts' posts.

**Rules:**
- Add a specific number, ratio, or data point they didn't include
- Never "great point!" — always add value
- Target accounts in the 50K-500K range in your niche (macro, crypto, DeFi)
- Identify 10-15 target accounts and check their posts daily (20 min)
- Your reply should make people click your profile

**Example:** Someone posts "BTC looking strong." You reply: "3.8% of gold's market cap. If that gap closes to 10%, that's 2.6x from here. The math hasn't changed, just the sentiment."

### 2. Data Cards as Scroll-Stoppers

2-3 visual data cards per week using the Alpine Patience aesthetic (dark backgrounds, geological data strata, minimal text).

- Use existing HTML templates from `naly X/` directory, swap in fresh data
- Every card should be screenshottable and self-contained
- Include @defi_naly watermark so shares credit you
- Format: one ratio, one implication, one visual bar

### 3. Strategic Quote Tweets

1-2 per week. Not "this is interesting" retweets. Add the contrarian angle or the math nobody did.

**Formula:** Original tweet + your framework + a specific number = value-add QT.

### 4. "Conviction Stack" Pinned Tweet

Your top 3 asymmetric bets with the math. Updated quarterly.

**Format:**
```
What I'm accumulating (Q1 2025):

BTC: 3.8% of gold. If gap closes to 10% → 2.6x. To 25% → 6.5x.
ZEC: 0.27% of BTC. Privacy reprices or it doesn't. 3.6-36x.
HYPE: 17.5% of Binance perp volume. On a DEX. Own the venue.

Full breakdown: [Substack link]

Not advice. Just math.

This is your storefront. Everyone who visits your profile sees it.

### 5. Community Flywheel

Genuine engagement in Zcash, Hyperliquid, and macro communities. Not shilling. Contributing.

- Participate in governance discussions
- Share original analysis in community channels
- Build relationships with project contributors
- This generates organic amplification from community accounts

### 6. naly.dev Tools as Content

Your interactive tools (Decay, Echo, The Fork, The Line, Saeculum, TruValue) are unique content assets nobody else has.

- Screenshot tool outputs with interesting inputs → post with commentary
- "I ran [scenario] through [tool name]. Here's what it shows:" format
- Link to interactive version for engagement
- This drives traffic to naly.dev AND creates unique X content

### 7. Collaborative Amplification

Build relationships with 10-15 accounts in the 10K-100K range. These are your growth peers.

- Engage genuinely with their content (not transactional)
- Share their work when it's genuinely good
- DM thoughtfully (share data, not asks)
- Over 3-6 months, mutual amplification compounds

### 8. Substack Cross-Pollination

Use each platform to feed the other:

- **X → Substack:** Every article gets a launch tweet + 3-5 derivative tweets that week. Bio link always points to Substack or a linktree with Substack prominent.
- **Substack → X:** Every article ends with "Daily takes on X: @defi_naly." Embed your best tweets in articles as social proof.
- **Substack Notes:** Cross-post tweet-length takes on Substack Notes for discovery within the Substack ecosystem.
- **Substack Recommendations:** Recommend other newsletters in your niche. They often reciprocate. This is the single fastest organic Substack growth lever.

## 6. Experimentation Framework

One variable at a time. Minimum 10 data points before drawing conclusions. Log everything in `experiments.md`.

### Experiment Format

```
Hypothesis: [If I do X, then Y will happen because Z]
Variable: [The one thing being changed]
Control: [What stays the same]
Duration: [Minimum 10 posts or 2 weeks]
Metric: [What you're measuring]
Result: [After experiment completes]
Ruling: [Keep / Kill / Modify]
```

### Priority Experiments (First 3 Months)

1. **Posting time:** Morning (7-9am) vs lunch (12-1pm) vs evening (6-8pm). Measure impressions.
2. **Visual vs text-only:** Do data cards outperform pure text tweets? Measure bookmarks + follows.
3. **Opening hook style:** Question vs statement vs number-first. Measure engagement rate.
4. **Question endings:** Do tweets ending with a question get more replies? Measure reply count.
5. **Substack subject lines:** Framework name vs question vs number-driven. Measure open rate.
6. **Thread vs single tweet:** For the same idea, which drives more followers? Measure profile visits.

### Rules

- Only run 1-2 experiments simultaneously
- Don't abandon an experiment early because one post flopped
- Write up results even when they're boring. "No difference" is data.
- Share interesting experiment results as content (meta-content about growing performs well)

**Pillar:** The Math
**Format:** Single tweet or data card
**Use:** 2-3x/week

```

If that gap closes to [Y]% → [multiplier]x.

**Example:**
```
Zcash: $3.53B market cap.

If privacy reprices to 1% of BTC → 3.6x.
If 5% → 18x.

You're betting floor, not ceiling.
```

### Template 2: "The Signal"

**Pillar:** The Signal
**Format:** Tweet or short Substack post
**Use:** 2-3x/week (especially around macro events)

**Structure:**
```
[Macro event or data point — specific, sourced]

Consensus take: [what everyone is saying]

What the data actually shows: [your framework, with numbers]

[Named framework or mental model]
```

**Example:**
```
CPI came in at 3.2%. Timeline is celebrating "inflation is dead."

Consensus: rate cuts incoming, risk-on.

What the data shows: shelter inflation is still 5.4%. Strip that out and sure, progress. Leave it in and you're still losing purchasing power at 2x the Fed's target.

This is the "good news that isn't" pattern. The number improved. The problem didn't.
```

**Format:** Tweet or Substack section
**Use:** 1x/week

[Cinematic setup — paint the scene]

[Verifiable historical detail with specifics]

[Implication for today]

2016. Six people in six cities. Each holding a shard of a cryptographic key that could compromise an entire network.

Air-gapped machines. No contact between participants. Hardware destroyed afterward.

One of the six? Edward Snowden. Operating under the codename "John Dobbertin."

When the guy who exposed the NSA's global surveillance apparatus volunteers for your genesis ceremony, that tells you something about the technology.

## 9. Brand Alignment Checklist

Run this before publishing anything. Takes 60 seconds.

### Voice Check (from `voice-guide.md`)

- [ ] Would I actually say this out loud?
- [ ] Does this sound like me after two drinks, talking to a smart friend?
- [ ] Is there at least one line someone would screenshot?
- [ ] Did I admit something real about myself? (Not required for every post, but check.)
- [ ] Is the ending quotable?

### Style Check (from `ARTICLE-STYLE.md`)

- [ ] No em dashes (use colons, parentheses, periods, or semicolons)
- [ ] No "it's not X, it's Y" constructions
- [ ] No AI-speak (delve, dive, unpack, leverage, unlock, empower, navigate)
- [ ] No throat-clearing ("It's worth noting...", "Interestingly...", "Let's dive in...")
- [ ] Specific over vague (numbers, names, dates instead of "many," "soon," "significant")
- [ ] No hedge-y qualifiers ("it seems like," "perhaps," "it could be argued")

### Visual Check (from `design-philosophy.md`)

- [ ] Alpine Patience aesthetic for any graphics (dark backgrounds, geological strata)
- [ ] Data as horizontal bands showing proportion (gold against stone)
- [ ] Minimal text on graphics (carved inscription, not floating labels)
- [ ] Chromatic recession: warm foreground → cool distance
- [ ] Museum-quality execution. If it looks like a Canva template, kill it.

### Tone Calibration

- [ ] Arena, not sidelines (you trade this stuff, you're not a commentator)
- [ ] Sardonic, not cynical (humor with warmth, not bitterness)
- [ ] Honest, not humble-bragging ("I lost money" not "I almost made millions")
- [ ] Profanity as punctuation, not decoration (one "fuck" hits harder than five)
- [ ] Conviction without certainty ("the math says" not "this will definitely")

## 11. Monetization Runway (The Clock)

You have 12 months. This section is the plan for making the audience pay for itself.

### Months 1-3: Build. Don't Monetize.

Resist the urge. A "buy my course" link at 14K followers with a stalled Substack screams desperation. Focus entirely on cadence, content quality, and audience growth.

**What you're building:** Trust, a content library, a reputation for doing the math.

### Months 4-6: Test + Signal

Start signaling expertise in ways that attract opportunities without directly selling.

- **Substack paid tier:** Launch with 1-2 exclusive posts/month. "The Math" deep dives with specific portfolio positioning. Price low ($7-10/month). Goal: prove willingness to pay, not revenue.
- **naly.dev as portfolio:** Your interactive tools are proof of capability. Link to them in bios, replies, and articles. They demonstrate you can build, not just write.
- **Consulting signals:** Drop occasional references to "working with a founder on X" or "helped someone model Y." Don't sell. Signal availability through demonstrated work.

### Months 7-9: Revenue Experiments

Based on what the audience responds to, test 2-3 of these:

| Revenue Stream | What | Target |
|----------------|------|--------|
| Substack paid tier | Exclusive "Math" deep dives, portfolio frameworks | $500-2K/month |
| Consulting/advisory | Crypto strategy for funds, protocols, or founders | $2-5K/month (1-2 clients) |
| Data card commissions | Custom Alpine Patience visuals for protocols/projects | $500-1K per project |
| Sponsored content | Aligned projects pay for analysis (disclosed, never shilled) | $1-3K per piece |
| Speaking/panels | Crypto conferences, podcasts | Exposure → consulting pipeline |

**Rule:** Never compromise the voice for money. If a sponsor wants you to shill, pass. The audience is worth more than any single deal.

### Months 10-12: Sustainable Model

By now you should have:
- 1-2 revenue streams generating $2-5K/month
- A clear path to $5-10K/month within 6 more months
- OR: a body of work that gets you hired into a role you actually want

**Fallback hierarchy:**
1. Paid Substack + consulting covers runway → keep going
2. Revenue is growing but not enough → extend runway if possible, double down on highest-ROI stream
3. Nothing is converting → your content library + audience is a resume. Leverage it into a role (crypto fund analyst, protocol comms, DeFi research)

The audience and the content library are never wasted. They're assets regardless of which path you take.
